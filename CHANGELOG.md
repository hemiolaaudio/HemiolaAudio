# Changelog

## 2.1.0

- New module: WHOA!, a two-operator FM voice with built-in effects, for techno, psytrance and dark ambient. The modulator and the carrier each have their own gate and ADSR, with a common gate, and a CV input on every envelope stage. Ratio, fine tuning, modulator feedback, drive, stereo spread, accent input, CV on index and feedback, and up to 16 voices.
- WHOA! also has a clocked delay (clock in, 16 note values, feedback, mix) and a reverb (Room, Hall, Vast, Shimmer, with pre-delay). The voices are summed before the effects, so LEFT and RIGHT are single stereo cables. CV depth for the voice, envelope and delay inputs is set in the right-click menu. 25 HP.
- Two example patches: Hemiola Whoa Dark Ambient and Hemiola Whoa Hypnotic Techno.
- Display text is larger and easier to read when the rack is zoomed out. The name and the figure share one line, and the line below is the same size, in a darker grey.
- Panel labels on Cycle Clock, R&T and WHOA! are larger and heavier, and the switch labels (ON, OFF, COUNT, LEVEL) are darker. Each letter is now drawn as its own shape, which stops Rack from filling in the holes in letters such as A, R and D.
- R&T shows "per beat, ÷ 11" when DIVIDE is above 1.
- README, About page and manuals rewritten in plainer language.

## 2.0.0

First release.

- Cycle Clock: 55 cycle presets, trigger and gate outputs for beat, section, cycle start and cycle end, position voltage, gate length, accented click, cycle choice by CV (Level or Count), tempo CV (1 V per octave), run input, and swing on the built-in clock.
- R&T (Rhythm & Thalam): splits each beat into 1 to 9 parts, trigger and gate outputs, gate length, accented click, division by CV (Level or Count), and a DIVIDE knob (1 to 64) with its own trigger and gate outputs for following fast nadai at a slower pace. CHANCE knob, RESET IN and DIVIDE CV. 10 HP.
- Light panel design with the Hemiola Audio mark on every module.
