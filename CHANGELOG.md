# Changelog

## 2.0.0

First release.

- Cycle Clock: 55 cycle presets, trigger and gate outputs for beat, section, cycle start and cycle end, position voltage, gate length, accented click, cycle choice by CV (Level or Count), tempo CV (1 V per octave), run input, and swing on the built-in clock.
- R&T (Rhythm & Thalam): splits each beat into 1 to 9 parts, trigger and gate outputs, gate length, accented click, division by CV (Level or Count), and a DIVIDE knob (1 to 64) with its own trigger and gate outputs for following fast nadai at a slower pace. CHANCE knob, RESET IN and DIVIDE CV. 10 HP.
- Light panel design with the Hemiola Audio mark on every module.
