# Contributing

Thank you for looking. Hemiola Audio is one person's project, so a clear note goes further than a large change.

## The most useful things you can do

1. **Play the modules and tell me what you find.** A patch that sounds wrong, a label that confuses you, a jack that does not do what you expected.
2. **Check a thalam.** If you teach or play Hindustani or Carnatic music and a cycle is grouped wrongly, use the "Thalam correction" form on the issues page. A link to a published source helps.
3. **Suggest a module.** Say what you would patch with it, not only what it should be.

## Sending a change

- Open an issue first for anything bigger than a typo, so we agree on it before you spend time.
- The timing rules are in `core/` and are tested by `tests/run_tests.sh`. A change there needs a test with it.
- Panels are generated by `tools/make_panels.py`. Do not edit the files in `res/` by hand, because they are overwritten.
- Keep new controls at the end of the lists in `src/`, so patches saved by earlier versions still open correctly.
